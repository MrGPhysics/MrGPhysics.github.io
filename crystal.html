How to Play Crystal Planet

  • Move: Use W A S D (or Arrow keys) and aim with the mouse.
  • Break Things: Left Click trees, rocks, crystals, or hunter traps to break them and collect shards.
  • Eat Cows & Pigs: Right Click on a cow or pig when nearby to eat it. Eat 5 to unlock your dragon's lightning or opal-transform power!
  • Dragon Power: Once unlocked, press Space or Right Click on objects/hunters to shoot crystal lightning or turn things into your dragon's crystal.
  • Armor & Inventory: Press E to open your inventory. Combine 5 items to craft armor, then equip it!
  • Glowing Scales: Press G to make your scales glow — super useful inside dark Crystal Caves!
  • Goal: Explore all 5 crystal biomes & caves, villages, and plains; grow to Level 5 (Elder Dragon) while escaping hunters and their traps!
