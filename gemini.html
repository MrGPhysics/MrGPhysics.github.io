AP Calculus AB: Essential Area & Volume Formulas

1. Area Between Curves
$$A = \int_{a}^{b} [top - bottom] \, dx$$

If curves are given in terms of $y$ ($x = f(y)$), integrate with respect to $y$: $\int_{c}^{d} [right - left] \, dy$.

2. Disk Method
$$V = \pi \int_{a}^{b} [R(x)]^2 \, dx$$

Used when the solid has no hollow core (region is flush against the axis of rotation $R(x)$).

3. Washer Method
$$V = \pi \int_{a}^{b} \left( [R(x)]^2 - [r(x)]^2 \right) dx$$

Used when there is a gap between the region and the axis of rotation ($R = \text{outer radius}$, $r = \text{inner radius}$).

4. Cylindrical Shell Method
$$V = 2\pi \int_{a}^{b} r(x) \cdot h(x) \, dx$$

Integrates parallel to the axis of rotation. $r(x)$ is radius from axis, $h(x)$ is height of shell.
