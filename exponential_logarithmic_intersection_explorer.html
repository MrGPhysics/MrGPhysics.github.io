Bifurcation Theory of a^x and log_a(x)

Click any regime below to jump parameter a
0 < a < e-e 3 Roots

Below e-e ≈ 0.06599, the curve crosses y = x once and spawns a symmetric pair (x₁, x₂) and (x₂, x₁) off the diagonal.

e-e ≤ a < 1 1 Root

Both functions are strictly decreasing and intersect at a single point along the line of symmetry y = x.

1 < a < e1/e 2 Roots

For bases slightly above 1 (up to e1/e ≈ 1.44467), the curves cross twice on y = x before ax overtakes log_a(x).

a = e1/e & a > e1/e 1 or 0 Roots

At a = e1/e, the curves kiss tangentially at (e, e). For a > e1/e, they never intersect.

Mathematical Expression Syntax Guide

You can type any mathematical expression in terms of variable x and dynamic base parameter a.
