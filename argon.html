AP Calculus AB Exam Concept Summary (Unit 8)

1. Area Between Two Curves (dx)

To find the area bounded by f(x) and g(x) on [a, b], integrate vertical slices of height (ytop - ybottom):

A = ∫ab [ ytop - ybottom ] dx
2. Washer Method around Horizontal Line y = k

Revolving a vertical slice around y = k forms a washer with outer radius R(x) (distance from y = k to outer curve) and inner radius r(x):

V = π ∫ab ( [R(x)]² - [r(x)]² ) dx
3. Visualizing 3D Cross-Sections

Use the Sweep Angle θ slider to watch the flat 2D region revolve through space, and toggle Isolate 3D Slice to see how a single 2D rectangle Δx sweeps out a 3D washer!
